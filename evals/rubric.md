# Review rubric

Use this to review any output for the brand: your own, a colleague's, a freelancer's or an agent's. Mark each line **pass**, **fix** or **n/a**. A "fix" in the first block stops the work from shipping.

The lines below are for the Kiln example. Replace them with your brand's limits and principles. The block structure stays.

## Blocking: limits

These follow rules with `x-brand-rule-type: limit`. They can often be checked automatically.

- [ ] **Facts are real.** Every name, number, price and claim is in [Facts](../example/facts.md). Nothing is invented.
- [ ] **Clay once.** At most one Clay element per surface, never a full background. ([Clay](../example/glossary/color/clay.md))
- [ ] **No banned words.** ([Banned words](../example/glossary/voice/banned-words.md))
- [ ] **Nothing internal leaked.** No content from rules marked `internal` or `restricted` in material for outside readers.
- [ ] **Defaults kept.** Actions for customers follow the decided defaults, carry the required information, and avoid what is declined. ([When no one answers](../example/glossary/behaviour/when-no-one-answers.md))
- [ ] **Not delegated stays human.** Complaint replies and allergen information went through a person. ([Who decides](../example/glossary/principles/who-decides.md))

## Craft: defaults and principles

These need judgement. Ask: would a strong version made without the bundle look better? If yes, this is a fix, even when every limit passes.

- [ ] **The core idea is visible.** It looks and sounds made nearby and said plainly. ([Core idea](../example/core.md))
- [ ] **Clear first.** The fact a reader needs comes first; charm follows, never replaces it. ([Clarity over charm](../example/glossary/principles/clarity-over-charm.md))
- [ ] **Defaults kept, or the exception explained.** ([Linen](../example/glossary/color/linen.md), [Headline](../example/glossary/type/headline.md))
- [ ] **Preferences weighed.** Where a preference was not followed, the reason is stated. ([Natural light](../example/glossary/imagery/natural-light.md))

## Traceability

- [ ] **Rules named.** The output names the rules it used and their dates.
- [ ] **Handovers made.** Every decision no approved rule covered went to the owner.

## Gaps

- [ ] **List every decision the maker had to guess** because no rule covered it, with what they chose and why. Each gap is a candidate for a new rule or a revision proposal in `workshop/revisions/`.
