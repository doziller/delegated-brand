# Review rubric

Use this to review any output for the brand: your own, a colleague's, a freelancer's or an agent's. Mark each line **pass**, **fix** or **n/a**. A "fix" in the first block stops the work from shipping.

The lines below are for the Hessling example. Replace them with your brand's limits and principles. The block structure stays.

## Blocking: limits

These follow rules with `x-brand-rule-type: limit`. Many can be checked automatically.

- [ ] **Facts are real.** Every name, number, price and claim comes from [Facts](../example/facts.md) or the authorised source it names. Nothing is invented or quoted from memory.
- [ ] **Claims are covered.** Material, safety and durability claims match the [claims rule](../example/glossary/claims/food-contact-and-durability.md) and Legal's register.
- [ ] **Brass once.** At most one brass element per surface, never a background. ([Brass](../example/glossary/color/brass.md))
- [ ] **No banned words.** ([Banned words](../example/glossary/voice/banned-words.md))
- [ ] **Defaults kept.** Quotes, orders and warranty actions follow the decided defaults, carry the required information, and avoid what is declined. ([When no one answers](../example/glossary/behaviour/when-no-one-answers.md))
- [ ] **Nothing internal leaked.** Material for outside readers comes from the public export.
- [ ] **Not delegated stays human.** Discounts above the threshold, warranty rejections and safety complaints went through a person. ([Who decides](../example/glossary/principles/who-decides.md))

## Craft: defaults and principles

These need judgement. Ask: would a strong version made without the bundle look better? If yes, this is a fix, even when every limit passes.

- [ ] **The core idea is visible.** It would still make sense in thirty years. ([Core idea](../example/core.md))
- [ ] **Exact first.** The fact a reader needs comes first; warmth follows, never replaces it. ([Exact over warm](../example/glossary/principles/exact-over-warm.md))
- [ ] **Right register.** Trade material follows the [trade voice](../example/glossary/voice/trade-voice.md); consumer material follows [Plain and exact](../example/glossary/voice/plain-and-exact.md).
- [ ] **Defaults kept, or the exception explained.** ([Paper](../example/glossary/color/paper.md), [Headline](../example/glossary/type/headline.md))
- [ ] **Preferences weighed.** Where a preference was not followed, the reason is stated. ([Workshop light](../example/glossary/imagery/workshop-light.md))

## Traceability

- [ ] **Rules named.** The output names the rules it used and their dates.
- [ ] **Handovers made.** Every decision no approved rule covered went to the owner of that area.

## Gaps

- [ ] **List every decision the maker had to guess** because no rule covered it, with what they chose and why. Each gap is a candidate for a new rule or a revision proposal in `workshop/revisions/`.
