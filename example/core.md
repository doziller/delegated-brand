# The idea

Kiln makes ceramics upstairs and serves coffee downstairs. The brand shows the making and says it plainly. Nothing is dressed up to look bigger, older or more refined than it is.

# In practice

- Colour comes from the objects. The surfaces stay quiet ([Linen](glossary/color/linen.md)), and only one thing per surface is loud ([Clay](glossary/color/clay.md)).
- Words come from the counter. [Plain language](glossary/voice/plain-language.md) wins over clever lines ([Clarity over charm](glossary/principles/clarity-over-charm.md)).
- Photos show real hands, real tables, daylight ([Natural light](glossary/imagery/natural-light.md)).

# When no rule covers the case

1. Make the plainest honest version first: the real fact, in a short sentence, on a quiet surface.
2. Ask what a regular would need to know first, and put that first.
3. Add one warm touch only if it does not make the message less clear.
4. If you still are not sure, hand the question to the owner. Do not decide alone.

# What it is not

Not rustic. Plain is not unfinished: the type is set carefully, the photos are sharp, the facts are exact.

# Why

People come to Kiln because they know the people and see the work. A brand that looks like a chain loses the reason they come.
