# Preference

Daylight, real tables, real hands. Weigh it against the job: a product shot for the online shop may need a plain studio setup, and that is fine.

# Why

Kiln's ceramics are glazed by hand, and daylight shows the glaze as it is. Studio lighting makes them look mass-produced.

This is a preference, not a limit. An agent weighs it and says when it chose otherwise.
