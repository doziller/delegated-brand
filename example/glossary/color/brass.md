# When

One element per surface: the action we want, or the one number that matters. "Request a quote". "40 years of spare parts". On packaging, the crank illustration.[^brand-book]

# When not

- Never as a background. Brass on large areas looks like gold, and Hessling is not a luxury brand.
- Never for text below 18 px. It fails contrast on Paper.
- Never for warnings or errors. Those come from the design system.

# Why

Every Hessling grinder has a brass crank. It is the part people touch. On a page, brass marks the part people should act on.

# Rejected

- **A brass gradient for a "premium" look.** Tested on packaging in 2024. Dealers said it looked like a gift set, not a tool.
- **Brass for every button.** With several brass elements, none stood out.
