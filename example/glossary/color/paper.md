# When

Behind everything: web, print, packaging, presentations. Product photos sit on Paper, never on white.

# When not

- Not inside the grinders' manuals, which are printed on recycled stock with no background colour.

# Why

White makes cast iron look cold. Paper keeps the warmth of the workshop without adding a colour.
