# When

Behind everything, unless a page needs Clay for one element. Product photos sit on Linen, never on white.

# When not

- Not for packaging. Kraft paper is the packaging surface, and it is not a token.
- Not behind dense text tables. Use the design system's table surface there.

# Why

White looks clinical next to handmade ceramics. Linen keeps the warmth of the studio without adding a colour.
