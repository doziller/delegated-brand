# Rule

- A claim about food contact, materials or safety is allowed only if Legal's certificate register lists it for that product and market.[^eu-1935-2004] Name the product, not the brand in general.
- Durability claims use only the numbers in [Facts](../../facts.md): 40 years of spare parts, 10 years warranty on the grinding mechanism.
- Never "food safe" as a general word. Say what was declared, for which part.

# When not

Internal training material may discuss pending certificates, marked as pending.

# Why

A wrong claim about food contact is a legal risk, not a style problem. Legal owns this rule; Brand only reviews that the wording stays plain.

[^eu-1935-2004]: Regulation (EC) No 1935/2004 on materials in contact with food
