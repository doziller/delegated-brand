# Rule

Short sentences. Concrete nouns. A number instead of an adjective whenever there is one. This holds for both audiences; the trade voice adds to it.

# Examples

| Instead of | Write |
| --- | --- |
| Our grinders offer exceptional longevity | Spare parts for every model, for at least 40 years |
| A truly premium grinding experience | Steel burrs, 38 mm, adjustable in 0.02 mm steps |

# Why

People who buy a Hessling compare. A number survives comparison; an adjective does not.

# Rejected

- **A warmer, storytelling voice for consumers only.** Tried in a 2025 campaign. Customers liked it, dealers could not reuse any of it. One voice with two registers works better than two voices.
