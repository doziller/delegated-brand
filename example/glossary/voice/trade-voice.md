# Rule

In quotes, product sheets and replies to trade customers, the order is fixed: specification, service and spare parts, delivery, price. Then, if at all, anything about heritage.

In German-speaking markets, address trade customers with "Sie". Consumers may be addressed with "du" in the online shop.

# When not

When a roastery's owner writes informally and personally, the reply may follow their tone. The order of information stays.

# Why

Roasteries buy a machine for ten years of daily use. They need to compare specifications quickly. Sales owns this rule because it decides what a quote must contain; Brand reviews that it stays plain and exact.

See [Plain and exact](plain-and-exact.md).
