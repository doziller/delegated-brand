# Rule

Short sentences, concrete nouns, no adjectives that only sound good. If the barista would not say it, we do not write it.

# Examples

| Instead of | Write |
| --- | --- |
| Experience our curated selection of artisanal ceramics | Mugs, bowls, and plates, all made upstairs |
| We are delighted to welcome you | Come in, the kettle is on |

# Why

Kiln is small and local. People come because they know the people. Polished copy makes it sound like a chain.

# Rejected

- **A formal "you" register for the online shop.** Proposed so the shop would feel more trustworthy. Dropped: regulars buy online too, and a different voice there made the shop feel like someone else's.
