# Priority

Kiln is warm and a little playful. When a joke or a pun makes a message harder to understand, the plain version wins.

# Example

A closure notice. Charming: "The kiln needs a nap." Clear: "Closed Monday 3 March. The kiln is being repaired. Open again Tuesday." The clear version goes on the door. The charming line may follow it, never replace it.

# Where it stops

On the menu chalkboard and in the newsletter sign-off, charm may lead. Nobody misses a train because of a pun on a chalkboard.

# Why

People who cannot find out when we are open stop coming. A brand that is charming but unclear loses regulars.

# Rejected

- **Charm first, everywhere.** The original founders' voice. Dropped after two seasons of questions at the counter about opening times.
